The payment state machine, laid out so a human can drive it.

This page is the payment layer's semantics made visible: the port's three operations, the rung vocabulary, the purchase and refund machines, the set record and its derivation, the conservation band and the acceptance gate — with a console you can drive to watch a set's state change, and the cases that must never be reachable shown as unreachable.

01

What this is

A single self-contained page that renders the payment layer's machine and lets you drive its set state. It is the aggregator's own vocabulary, drawn.

The wallet owns the set: it binds one frozen total to an ordered list of legs, keeps each leg's witness rather than a verdict, derives the set state from what is proven, enforces conservation over the aggregate, runs the acceptance gate every wrapper passes, and is the durable authority on whether a refund set exists for an order.

This page shows that machine and makes it interactive. It is a model: the rules are transcribed from the modules' semantics, and the console re-implements the same derivation the aggregator runs (deriveSetState) so you can watch it work. It is not a running payment system, and it is not evidence — the module's own suite and evidence are.

What it is

A model of the set's rules, a console that derives the set state live, and the machines laid out transition by transition.

What it is not

Not a ledger, not a host, not a rail. It holds no record, opens no network, and settles nothing.

No live network

Nothing here depends on a relay, a mint or a wallet. Turn JavaScript off and the page is still fully readable.

02

The port — three operations, and the subset a rail declares

One closed set of operations. A rail declares which of them it has; the tie is one-directional and asserts only self-consistency.

The funds contract declares one port over one rail, and it is a closed set of exactly three operations:

Operation What it does
obtain(intent) Returns the leg's durable reference in the rail's own form — an invoice and its payment hash, a mint's quote — or a fail-closed refusal.
submit(quote, reference) Hands the payable artifact to the rail so it moves money.
reconcile(reference) Asks the authority (through the wrapper) whether the leg settled, and returns a handle carrying the witness or its explicit absence.

A wrapper implements the port over one rail and owns its own durable journal. Its declared operations is a non-empty subset of those three, and its declared plan is that subset's ordered projection in the port's canonical order (obtain, then submit, then reconcile).

Which operation a rail may omit — and the one that may not be omitted

A passive leg is legal by this declaration. A rail whose payable artifact is broadcast — an invoice shown for the buyer to scan, with nothing in the app that submits it — declares obtain and reconcile and omits submit. Its absent submit is then a declared fact, not a missing method. A rail that does declare submit (a leg that moves money) must also declare reconcile — a money-mover with no reconcile step is a paid but unlearnable leg, and the gate refuses it.

A money-moving rail

obtain · submit · reconcile

Moves money and can be learned.

A passive rail

obtain · reconcile

Omits submit; the artifact is broadcast, not submitted.

Refused

obtain · submit

A money-mover with no reconcile — refused as plan no-reconcile-step.

03

The rung vocabulary — cryptography settles, a word does not

Progress markers prove nothing about money. The proof rungs are named by the artifact they carry, not by the party that reports them.

A leg climbs progress markers before any proof exists; none of them is a settlement:

intent → attempted → submitted progress only

Then it carries one of four proof rungs:

Rung What it proves
witnessed-settled A cryptographic artifact checkable against the leg's own expectation (the invoice's payment hash, the mint's quote state). The strongest true statement: this leg settled.
attested A party's or authority's word, with no artifact behind it. Reconciliation is credited no further than here.
unresolved No witness yet, and one may still arrive. Open — a blocked, unconfirmed leg is a sub-state of this.
abandoned The window elapsed and no artifact will arrive. Closed non-settled.

Witness kinds — cryptographic versus declared

A witness is named by what can be forged. Two families, and only one may settle:

Cryptographic may settle

  • preimage — checked as SHA256(preimage) against the invoice's own payment_hash.
  • zap_receipt — a signed receipt whose preimage tag validates.
  • mint_quote_state — a state the wrapper re-derives from a local mint.

Checkable without trusting the reporter: the proof lives in the artifact.

Declared never settles

  • lnurl_verify — a server answering settled with no preimage.
  • seller_attestation — the payee's own confirmation.
  • ack — a wallet's bare acknowledgement.

A reporter's word. It may move a leg to attested and no further.

The honest limit this shows rather than hides

A declared witness — even the payee's own node saying the money arrived — reaches only the weaker rung attested. A leg is settled on an artifact checkable against its own expectation, never on a report. And the report that must never be read as settlement is the one whose reporter gains from the reading: the payer's acknowledgement (they get goods without paying) is beneficiary-favourable; the payee's confirmation is beneficiary-adverse and is admitted as a bounded claim, never as settlement.

A passive leg cannot be witnessed by the app. A leg that omits submit broadcasts its artifact and never submits it, so the app cannot manufacture its witness — it can only learn of settlement if an artifact arrives (the buyer's own preimage, or a verify that returns one). Absent that, the window elapses and the leg is abandoned.

04

The set record — a witness becomes a rung, rungs become a state

The record keeps the witness it saw, never a verdict. The set state is derived every time from what the legs prove.

The set record binds one frozen total to an ordered list of legs, and per leg it keeps the witness it saw — its kind, source, when it was observed, its reference — plus the leg's expectation (the payment hash the set already holds), its frozen share, and an untrusted marker. It is written before any leg is submitted.

leg's witness
→
a rung. A cryptographic kind that meets the expectation is witnessed-settled; a declared kind, or a cryptographic one that disagrees, is attested; none and the window open is unresolved; none and the window elapsed is abandoned.
the legs' rungs
→
the settled sum. A leg counts toward the settled sum only when it is witnessed-settled and not untrusted. An untrusted leg is excluded from the derivation and from its amounts.
the settled sum
→
the set state. All legs settled → complete, short-settled or over-settled by the conservation band. All legs terminal with at least one abandoned → abandoned. Otherwise unfinished.

The exclusion that stops an untrusted leg counting

A leg marked untrusted is dropped from the derivation and from the amounts. So a set can never be reported complete while one of its legs is untrusted: an untrusted leg is never settled, so the completeness condition is never satisfied. This is forbidden by construction, not by care.

The record holds each leg's witness expectation and the handle by which the owning wrapper is asked — never a copy of any leg's reference. A returned witness that disagrees with the stored expectation is surfaced as a mismatch and the set stays unfinished; the wrapper's leg is the leg's truth and the aggregator's expectation is the set's, and a disagreement is never merged into either.

05

The purchase machine — every transition, rule or human

The buyer pays the seller. Every transition of this direction is reached by a rule; time is a rule input, not a human.

intent
→
the durable intent is written, the payable artifact exposed
attempted
→
the transport echo (an NWC request published, a bolt11 handed to a signer, a melt claimed)
submitted
→
an authority's word with no artifact — the attested rung
witnessed-settled
→
a cryptographic artifact validates against the leg's own expectation — terminal and write-once
abandoned
→
the settleability window elapsed — terminal, non-settled
Each transition of the purchase direction, and who reaches it.
Transition Reached by Why
intent → attempted rule The intent is journaled and the artifact exposed.
attempted → submitted rule The transport echo.
submitted → attested rule An authority's word, with no artifact.
{attempted, submitted, attested, unresolved} → witnessed-settled rule human input possible A cryptographic artifact validates. A QR leg reaches this directly on the buyer's hand-entered preimage — a human-supplied input, but the artifact that closes it is cryptographic.
{attempted, attested, unresolved} → abandoned rule The settleability window elapsed. Time alone reaches the non-settlement terminal — it can never write witnessed-settled.
unfinished → complete rule All legs settled, sum inside the aggregate band.
unfinished → abandoned rule Every leg terminal, at least one abandoned.
abandoned → complete rule A late artifact promotes the last leg and the sum is whole.

The contract states that a transition is human-reached if and only if its only trigger is a human-supplied input, and that there are exactly three in the whole layer: the buyer's manual preimage (a hand-entered preimage validated against the leg's own expectation); the seller's confirmation moving a leg to the declared non-settlement rung; and the seller opening a refund set. Every other transition — the time-reached terminal included — is rule-reached.

06

The refund machine — the one transition that needs a human

The refund is the same machine run backwards, as a separate set. Its initiation is the layer's single human transition; everything after it is reached by a rule.

A refund is a separate set whose payee is the buyer — a seller-side second payment, never a mutation of the purchase set. It names the purchase set it reverses, and its legs are the same kinds, so the same rung reachability applies.

attempted · submitted · attested | unresolved

the same rail calls as the purchase direction rule
witnessed-settled
→
a cryptographic artifact validates — settles the refund leg rule
Transition Reached by Why
— → refund set opened human The seller opens a refund set for an abandoned order. The market has no automated refund path and no dispute channel, so the trigger is off-protocol; the human is the seller.
record → intent rule The refund set record is written before any refund leg moves money.
intent → attempted → submitted → attested | unresolved → witnessed-settled rule The same rail calls and the same cryptographic settle as the purchase direction.
refund leg with no witness → abandoned rule The refund leg's own unresolved terminal — the last money-moving act cannot sit in limbo.
refund set → complete | abandoned rule The same conservation and terminal rules as the purchase set.

The refund guard — a second refund set for one order is unreachable

The aggregator's durable store is the sole authority on whether a refund set exists for an order. openRefund resolves the named purchase set and keys on that resolved set's own identity — never on an identifier the caller supplies unchecked. Before writing any refund record or calling any wrapper it refuses, fail-closed: a duplicate (a refund set already exists for the resolved order), an unresolvable correlation (the named purchase set does not exist), and a refund whose frozen total exceeds the purchase total.

07

The conservation band — and the two directions a settled set can take

A per-leg band, summed for the aggregate. A fully settled set is never reported incomplete; a settled set beyond the band is short or over, never one state and never abandoned.

The basis is net, and the residual has an owner: the aggregate is derived from the parts — the frozen total is the sum of the legs' frozen shares, fees are netted before the comparison, and the merchant's share is the residual by construction.

Each settled leg's observed amount is compared against that leg's own frozen share with a per-leg band. The band that applies to the aggregate is the sum of the per-leg bands, N × tolerance, by the triangle inequality — never a flat per-leg bound applied to a sum.

Inside the band complete

Every leg settled and the sum within N × tolerance of the frozen total: the set is complete.

A fully settled set is impossible to report as incomplete — that is a case that must never be reachable.

Beyond the band — two directions, never one

A fully settled set beyond the aggregate band takes the direction its signed residual implies:

  • short-settled — the residual falls below the band; the merchant is owed (under-collected).
  • over-settled — the residual rises above the band; the merchant holds extra (over-collected).

Two states, never one, and neither is abandoned.

08

The acceptance gate — what it refuses, and its honest limit

Every wrapper is accepted or refused at registration. The gate refuses fail-closed from two closed enums that interpolate no value.

A wrapper hands the aggregator a declaration. The gate checks the shape the contract owns and refuses, fail-closed, with one error constructed from two closed enums — a field and a reason — that echoes no value, so no payload can reach the message and a value with a hostile toString cannot turn a refusal into another error.

Field Reasons it may name
wrapperId missing · empty · too-long · untrimmed · control-character
method empty · too-long · untrimmed · control-character · duplicate-method
authorityKind not-in-union
operations empty · not-in-union
plan empty · unknown-step · duplicate-step · no-reconcile-step · wrong-projection
witnessKinds empty · not-in-union
store · storeKind empty
reconcileDeadlineMs not-positive-integer
journalReference incomplete · missing · not-in-union
ceiling not-positive-integer
contractVersion stale-version

The gate's honest limit, stated once so the spec does not overclaim

It enforces the shape and self-consistency of a declaration, never its conduct. It cannot make a declared plan the plan that runs, make reconcile truthful, make a declared witness producible, make a store durable or its compare-and-set atomic, or make a ceiling the cart's own number. A well-formed declaration that lies is accepted. So the gate is worth building — a malformed registration is a real failure mode — but the behavioural enforcement the design wants is the conformance suite, a deliverable. The gate refuses a wrapper with no reconciliation in its plan; whether a wrapper can produce the witness kinds it declares is not mechanically checkable, and the suite, not the gate, carries that obligation.

10

What can never happen

These are shown as unreachable rather than asserted: the console above will not let you drive the set into any of them.

  • ✕
    A set reported complete (or otherwise paid) while one of its legs is untrusted.
    An untrusted leg is excluded from the derivation and from its amounts, so it is never settled and the completeness condition is never satisfied. Try scenario 9: mark a settled leg untrusted and the state falls back to unfinished.
  • ✕
    A fully settled set, every leg within its own per-leg band, reported as anything but complete.
    When every leg is reconciled to a settlement and the sum is inside the aggregate band, the derivation can only produce complete. Try scenario 3.
  • ✕
    A leg settled on a declared witness, or on a cryptographic witness that disagrees with the expectation.
    A declared kind, or a mismatch, moves a leg no further than attested. Try scenario 8 (a seller attestation) or withhold the "matches expectation" box under a preimage.
  • ✕
    Time alone writing a settlement.
    The deadline is a wait bound and the trigger for the non-settlement terminal, never a settleability bound. Let the window elapse with no witness and the leg becomes abandoned, never witnessed-settled.
  • ✕
    A second refund set for one order.
    The refund guard refuses a duplicate on the resolved order's own identity, so it is unreachable by construction.
